Auto expressions: sensitivity
Needs Auto expressions from your face (Tracking). The bars show what's read from your face right now; the strongest one past the mark shows on your avatar. Raise one that never triggers, lower one that triggers too easily. Calibrating with a relaxed face helps a lot. If one never goes all the way, Calibrate it: make that face the way you normally would, click, hold it for 2 seconds; that becomes its full strength.
